White background or lifestyle: which photo sells first

2026-11-21 Photography

What the buyer actually does

On a product page, the first three seconds are spent confirming the object exists in the shape they expected. A clean white-background shot answers that fastest.

The order that works

  • Hero: white background, straight-on, soft shadow
  • Detail: close crop on the material or the seam
  • Context: one lifestyle frame, real use, no props

Practical notes

Shoot at 1600 px on the long edge, export as JPEG under 400 KB. Anything larger slows the page and buys nothing.
