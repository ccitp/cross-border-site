Three MOQ mistakes that cost you the order

2026-11-17 Sourcing

Mistake one: quoting MOQ before the channel

A distributor and a test-order brand need different numbers. Ask what the first order is for before naming a quantity.

Mistake two: one MOQ for every colour

Splitting a 500-piece order across four colours is a different production run. Price it that way instead of absorbing it.

Mistake three: treating MOQ as fixed

A 300-piece trial at a higher unit price beats a 1,000-piece order that never ships.

Name the trial price openly. It converts far more often than a discount nobody asked for.
