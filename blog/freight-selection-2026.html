Freight selection notes for small cross-border orders

2026-11-09 Logistics

The threshold that matters

Below roughly 2 CBM, the fixed costs of sea freight — documentation, port handling, destination clearance — dominate. The per-kilo rate is a distraction.

A working rule

  • Under 0.5 CBM: air or express consolidation
  • 0.5-2 CBM: LCL, if the buyer can wait four weeks
  • Over 2 CBM: sea freight starts to pay for itself

What to confirm before quoting

Destination port, whether the buyer clears customs themselves, and whether the delivery address takes a 40-foot container. Getting any of these wrong moves the number by more than the freight itself.
