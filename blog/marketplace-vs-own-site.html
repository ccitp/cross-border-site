Marketplace and your own site: why both

2026-11-13 Strategy

What each one is actually good at

A marketplace brings buyers who are already searching. Your own site brings the buyer who was sent there by a colleague, a trade show card, or a quote thread.

The split that holds up

  • Marketplace: discovery, price comparison, small repeat orders
  • Own site: specifications, custom programmes, sample requests
  • Both: the same product names, the same spec values

Why a static site is enough

A spec page and an inquiry button are the whole job. No cart, no login, no database. That keeps the page fast and the maintenance near zero.
