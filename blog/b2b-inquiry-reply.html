How to answer a B2B inquiry in one message

2026-11-24 Sales

The four questions behind every inquiry

A buyer writing "what's your price for 500 pcs" is usually asking four things at once: unit price at that quantity, what is included, how long it takes, and whether you can actually do it.

  • Price at the stated quantity, not a range
  • What's in it — packaging, certification, samples
  • Lead time from deposit, not from order
  • Next step — one clear action

Keep it to one screen

A reply that fits one screen gets answered. A reply with an eight-row table and three attachments gets filed for later.

Answer all four in the first message. Save the catalogue for the second.
